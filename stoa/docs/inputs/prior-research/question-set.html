What Stoa asks about drawings

The Stoa "Make Blueprints Talk" preview dataset: real drawings with quantity-takeoff questions and reference answers. Counts must be exact. Areas, lengths and volumes must be within 2%. Text answers are free-form.

Questions per source and type

Most questions are counts on Finnish floor-plan images. Areas come from Swiss plans and IFC models. Text answers come from IFC-Bench.

Language and wording

Each question is usually asked twice: in English and in the drawing's local language, with the same answer. Wording comes from a small set of templates.

  • English4,088
  • Finnish2,199
  • German422
  • French395
  • Italian374
  • Estonian315
  • Danish197
  • 402×What is the net floor area of the room '…' on '…', in m²?
  • 392×How many rooms labelled '…' are there on this floor?
  • 241×How many windows are shown on this floor plan?
  • 241×How many floors (storeys) does this floor plan show?
  • 240×How many toilet bowls (WC seats) are drawn on this floor plan?
  • 239×How many sinks and wash basins (kitchen and bathroom combined; a double-bowl sink counts as one) are drawn on this floor plan?
  • 227×How many showers and bathtubs are drawn on this floor plan in total?
  • 176×How many doors are shown on this floor plan? Count doorways fitted with a door; open doorways without a door leaf do not count.
  • 165×How many saunas are on this floor plan?
  • 161×How many kitchens (including kitchenettes, open kitchens and utility kitchens) are on this floor plan?
  • 154×How many bedrooms (makuuhuone, usually marked MH) are on this floor plan?
  • 127×What is the net floor area of room N ('…') in m²?

Most common English templates. Numbers become N, quoted names become '…'.
